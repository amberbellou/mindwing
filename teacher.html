Set up a class

Mindwing is a short game about how AI works and how to keep your mind your own. Make a class and you get two links: one for your students, and a private dashboard for you. No accounts, and no student names.

Shown to students on the game's title screen. Please do not use any student's name.

What you will see

For the whole class: how far students got, how often they were hit on each level, how long they read each lesson, how many quick-check answers were right, and how well they told real answers from hallucinated ones in the Mirage Marsh.

For each run: the three-letter initials a student typed on the leaderboard (if they signed it), the difficulty, the furthest level, their score, their quick-check results and their answer-orb choices. Initials are not names, but in a small class they can point to a child, so treat the dashboard as class records.

Levels

Reached: share of runs that started the level. Hits per try: how often students were hit per attempt. Lesson read: average seconds on the lesson card before the level.

Quick checks

Two questions after each lesson. A low score on one question is a good place to start a class discussion.

Before and after

The same five questions are asked before playing and again after the Engine falls, and they are never scored in the game. Only students who finished have an "after" score, so compare the two columns with that in mind.

Runs

One row per game started, newest first. Initials appear only when a student signed the leaderboard. Quick checks below half right are highlighted.

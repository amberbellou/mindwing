# Fonts

Both typefaces are self-hosted here so the game makes no third-party request and works offline. Both are released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

- **Nunito** (body text), by Vernon Adams, Cyreal, Jacques Le Bailly. https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Nunito
- **Audiowide** (title, headings, level banners), by Astigmatic. https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Audiowide

The files are the Latin subsets served by Google Fonts, unmodified. The OFL text is at https://openfontlicense.org/.
